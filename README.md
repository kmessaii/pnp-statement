# AI labs should stop working on P = NP

Draft statement and the research behind it.

Live draft: https://pnp-draft-bb8da4.pages.dev/

## What is here

- `site/` is the page as served: one HTML file, one stylesheet, and the STIX Two fonts (SIL Open Font License, text in `site/fonts/`). It makes no requests to any other server.
- `research/` is the background reading.
  - `precedents.md`: 25 cases where research was paused, withheld or renounced, and how each turned out.
  - `what_is_dangerous.md`: what a practical method would and would not break, and where AI mathematics stood in November 2026.
  - `letters_cryptographers_signed.md`: open letters cryptographers have signed, and what the successful ones had in common.
  - `source_check.md`, `source_check_2.md`: two independent checks of the page's claims against their sources.
  - `hard_readers.md`: the page read as a complexity theorist, an applied cryptographer and a lab policy lead would read it.

## Status

The research files were compiled with AI assistance. They check earlier versions of the page; its argument has been rewritten since, and the current text has not been checked again. No mathematician or cryptographer has reviewed any of it yet. Quotations should be checked against their sources before reuse.
